## More than a resume

I've shipped projects, published research, and TA'd courses — but those facts don't capture *who* I am or how I think.

## What this site is for

- A home base that grows with me
- A place to write without algorithmic noise
- A clearer story for recruiters **and** people who want to know the whole person

## What comes next

Project deep dives, a `/now` page, interests, and the community work that never fits neatly on LinkedIn.

## The technical part

We built a quiz platform with sticky sessions using Redis and Node.js — real concurrency problems, real tradeoffs.

## The human part

The harder lessons were about communication: scoping work, unblocking teammates, and keeping Agile lightweight instead of ceremonial.

## The takeaway

The best teams aren't the ones with the flashiest stack. They're the ones where people know what's happening and why it matters.
